## 3.3 One deviation from people_stream's copy, named because it is one

people_stream's `install!` has two direct prepends outside `declare_target`;
**this one has three**. The third (`concolic_targets.rb:1565`, BOUNDARY
HARVEST B-2) prepends `ConcolicEscapeSegmentShim` onto
`ActionDispatch::Journey::Router::Utils.singleton_class` — and the
`escape_segment` shim test in this very file measures the REAL escaper's body,
which it can do today only because the shim rig never ran `install!`. Letting
that prepend land would have left the entry green while silently re-pointing
its `coverage_of` at the overlay's two lines: a pass over a different quantity,
the exact failure DISCIPLINE §14 keeps recording.

So the overlay is suppressed rather than tolerated. Its guard is
`Utils.respond_to?(:escape_segment)`, so the real singleton method is DETACHED
across the `install!` call — its `UnboundMethod` kept, the same "detach, keep,
re-attach" pattern as `SHIM_MEMO_BODY`, and an `UnboundMethod` carries its
original `source_location` — and re-attached under `ensure` immediately after.
The preamble then ASSERTS both halves: `ConcolicEscapeSegmentShim` absent from
the ancestry, and `escape_segment`'s `source_location` still inside actionpack.
Measured, not argued: that entry's verdict, `coverage_pct` (100.0) and
`missed_lines` are identical before and after the port (3.4).

Also enumerated in the preamble, so the next reader does not have to re-derive
it: the only other things `install!` does to a real class or to the global
namespace are `::Object.const_set(:ConcreteSymbolicString, …)` (a new constant,
shadowing nothing, named by no test here), and — at REQUIRE time, not in
`install!` — the two `ConcolicKwargsToPositional` prepends onto
`ActiveRecord::Base.singleton_class` and `ActiveRecord::Relation`. Those two
are the dispatch layer the `…DYNAMIC` entries already describe and test
through; the bodies they measure (`FinderMethods#find_by`,
`Core::ClassMethods#find_by!`) live on modules those prepends do not touch, so
both the bodies and the `TargetCallProbe` captures are unchanged — confirmed
by the same before/after comparison.

**The order dependence is real and is recorded here rather than engineered
around.** `TargetCallProbe.capture` resolves its "original" through a prepend,
so once the memo body is re-attached inside its own fixture, any LATER entry
that both declares `SingularAssociation#find_target` and calls it would
recurse into a JVM StackOverflow. On this batch the three entries that call it
(`Person.singleton_class.find_or_fetch_by_identifier`, `CommentPresenter.as_json`,
